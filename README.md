# 📖 Project Overview

Supply chains generate large volumes of operational data every day. Transforming this data into actionable insights is essential for improving inventory planning, supplier performance, manufacturing efficiency, logistics, and overall business profitability.

This end-to-end analytics project demonstrates how SQL and Power BI can be used together to analyze supply chain operations and build an interactive business intelligence dashboard for decision-makers.

The project follows a complete analytics workflow—from data preparation and SQL analysis to dashboard development and business recommendations.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

The objective of this project was to answer key business questions including:

- Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which suppliers contribute the most to business performance?
- Which products are at risk of inventory shortages?
- Which transportation methods are most efficient?
- Which suppliers have the highest defect rates?
- How do manufacturing costs differ across product categories?
- How can operational performance be improved?

# 💡 Key Insights

- Skincare products generated the highest revenue.
- Revenue was concentrated among a few suppliers.
- Some products showed high demand despite lower inventory levels.
- Shipping performance varied across transportation carriers.
- Manufacturing costs differed significantly across product categories.
- Supplier defect rates revealed opportunities for quality improvement.

---

# 📌 Business Recommendations

- Improve inventory planning for high-demand products.
- Monitor supplier performance using defect and revenue metrics.
- Optimize shipping carrier selection to reduce delivery time.
- Focus quality improvement efforts on suppliers with higher defect rates.
- Track manufacturing costs regularly to improve profitability.
- Build automated dashboards for continuous operational monitoring.
